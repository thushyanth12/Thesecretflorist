How to Make Your Rose Bouquet Last Longer

Pondicherry's coastal humidity is kind to roses if you follow a few simple steps. Keep the bouquet away from direct afternoon sun and air-conditioner drafts, trim 1–2 cm off the stems at an angle under running water every second day, and change the vase water daily. Mist the petals lightly in the evening for extra freshness.

Want the moment to last forever? Our preserved and eternal rose boxes keep their beauty for months without water — a favourite for proposals, wedding keepsakes, and Valentine's surprises. Ask us on WhatsApp when ordering and we will show you the current preserved-rose designs in stock.
