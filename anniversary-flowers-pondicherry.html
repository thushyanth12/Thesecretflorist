How to Choose the Perfect Anniversary Bouquet

Not sure where to start? Match the flowers to the message. Red roses remain the timeless language of passionate love. Pink roses and lilies express admiration and gratitude — lovely for partners and in-laws alike. White and gold arrangements suit milestone celebrations, while orchids and mixed exotics feel modern and sophisticated for couples who like something different.

Timing matters too. For surprise deliveries to workplaces in the city centre, we recommend a late morning slot; for home surprises, evening delivery just before dinner creates the perfect moment. Midnight anniversary delivery can often be arranged on request — message us early on WhatsApp to confirm availability for your date.
